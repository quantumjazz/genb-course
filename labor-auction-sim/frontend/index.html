Milgrom & Roberts · Chapter 8

A 15-minute experiment in wages, effort and shirking.

Each student plays a worker looking for a job. Bot-controlled firms post wages that adjust from round to round as they observe whether their workers put in effort. The class will watch aggregate wages, shirking, and unemployment emerge on a shared dashboard — then debrief the pattern afterwards.

How the session runs

  1. The instructor opens Instructor and creates the class session.
  2. Students open Student on any phone or laptop and enter their faculty ID plus a screen name.
  3. The instructor presses Start. Each round has three phases: an auction where students pick a job, an effort phase where they choose work hard or slack off, and a resolution phase.
  4. The Projection page shows live aggregate charts — point a browser at it from the classroom computer.
  5. At the end, the instructor downloads a CSV of every round and every choice.
